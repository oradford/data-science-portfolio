What is Data Science to Me?

	Learning data science in social and interdisciplinary (multiple branches of knowledge) contexts is important because it helps us have understanding of what is going on in the world and makes it easier to understand. An easy way that I like to think of things is by using sports. In this case, data science is important because it makes it easier to understand trends of the game. You can watch a whole football game as a coach and understand that your team is losing, but you might not understand exactly why. If you have assistant coaches on the sideline, you have access to a lot more information because of the fact that they have technology with statistics and data. Their respective technology could give you insight that your team runs the ball much better on first down and when you run on the goal line you’re more likely to score. This is important because it helps you receive insight that you might not have noticed. This specific example is rather basic, but data science has endless possibilities as to what you can do with it. Continuing with sports, there are coaches that are notorious for being very analytical. In fact, a large majority of the coaches that are so renowned for being analytical are very solid coaches. Funny enough, there are also coaches that are too analytical and it hurts them in the long run. Using data about people, you can gather their interests and their personality, as well as several other things that you might not have known just by talking to them. In this course, I’m hoping to understand how to better use this data to observe trends through graphs and charts. I have always loved sports as well as the analytical side of sports and I believe that it would go a very long way as far as developing my abilities and taking a step forward in my career.
